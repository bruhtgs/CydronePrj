# ADR 0003: Zero mandatory cost

- Status: Accepted
- Decision: No essential feature may require a paid API, subscription, proprietary hosted database, or paid hosting provider.
- Rationale: The project must remain usable without recurring expenditure.
- Consequence: Local/self-hosted alternatives are required. Free hosted tiers are optional conveniences, never foundational assumptions. Free tiers may impose quotas, sleep, or policy changes.
